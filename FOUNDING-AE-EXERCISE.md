**Selection logic (company + contacts):**  
I focused on **Food & Beverage, Cold Chain, and CPG** where Owlery already has references (Daily Harvest, Little Spoon, Local Bounti, Force of Nature). All 10 are mid-market brands with **high-volume or complex freight**—perishable, multi-SKU, DTC + retail, or multi-site. I chose **Director/VP of Supply Chain, Fulfillment, Logistics, or Operations** because they own carrier relationships, freight spend, and visibility; they're the same personas Owlery's testimonials use and the ones who feel spreadsheet/legacy-TMS pain most. I calibrated to Owlery's reference customers (ZoomInfo: Little Spoon ~$47M, Local Bounti ~$46M, Daily Harvest ~$127M, Force of Nature ~$6M).
