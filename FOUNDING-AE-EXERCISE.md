**Selection logic (company + contacts):**  
I focused on **Food & Beverage, Cold Chain, CPG, and adjacent consumer brands** where Owlery already has references (Daily Harvest, Little Spoon, Local Bounti, Force of Nature). All 10 are scaling or mid‑market shippers with **high‑volume or complex freight**—perishable product, multi‑SKU portfolios, DTC + retail channels, or multi‑site networks. For each account I built a small buying group of 2–3 people: a **senior supply chain / operations owner** (C/VP/SVP level), an **operations or fulfillment leader** (director/ops lead), and where relevant a **systems or transportation manager** (logistics systems, transportation, or supply chain excellence). These are the personas who own freight spend, visibility, and process change, and they mirror the titles from Owlery’s current customers and ZoomInfo benchmarks (Little Spoon ~$47M, Local Bounti ~$46M, Daily Harvest ~$127M, Force of Nature ~$6M).
