# Owlery — Current Customers & Industries (Website Investigation)

Summary of what’s on [owlery.ai](https://owlery.ai) and related sources: named customers, their industries, and Owlery’s stated focus industries.

---

## Named customers on the website

Owlery shows **four named customers** with quotes and titles. No other customer names appear on the main site. There are no dedicated /customers or /case-studies pages (those URLs 404); case study links exist but the blog pages return the same template as the homepage.

| Customer        | Title/role (quote)              | Industry (from research) | What Owlery highlights |
|----------------|----------------------------------|---------------------------|-------------------------|
| **Little Spoon** | Director of Strategic Operations | **Food & Beverage / CPG** — DTC baby & kids food subscription; fresh meals, retail (Target); ~$300M valuation | “Night and day” insight; integrations eliminated repetitive data entry; used during retail launch (30k+ meals/week) |
| **Daily Harvest** | VP Supply Chain                  | **Food & Beverage / CPG** — DTC frozen plant-based meals & smoothies; subscription + retail (Kroger, Target); acquired by Chobani 2025 | **25% freight cost reduction in first 3 months** |
| **Force of Nature** | Logistics Manager             | **CPG / Home goods** — Non-toxic cleaning (electrolyzed water); home + Pro/commercial; 1M+ customers; Inc. 5000 | Nothing falling through the cracks; saves a day of emails; prevents trucks from getting stranded |
| **Local Bounti** | Director of Logistics            | **Food & Beverage / Cold chain / CEA** — Indoor farming (controlled environment ag); living lettuce, herbs; NYSE (LOCL) | New technology, automation, data-driven decision making |

**Takeaway:** All four are **mid-market, high-growth brands** with complex freight (perishable, multi-site, or high SKU). Three are **Food & Beverage / CPG**; one is **CPG / home goods**. Personas are **Director/VP of Logistics, Supply Chain, or Strategic Operations** and **Logistics Manager**.

## For your AE exercise

- **Proven segments:** Food & Beverage, Cold Chain, CPG (and adjacent home goods). Your 10-account list can lean into similar brands (DTC + retail, perishable or multi-SKU, $50M–$500M scale).
- **Personas:** Director of Logistics, VP Supply Chain, Director of Strategic Operations, Logistics Manager — the same titles that appear in Owlery’s testimonials.
- **Proof points to reuse:** “25% freight cost reduction in first 3 months” (Daily Harvest), “night and day” visibility and less manual entry (Little Spoon), nothing falling through the cracks / trucks not stranded (Force of Nature), automation and data-driven decisions (Local Bounti).
- **Wider ICP:** Owlery also calls out Industrial, Manufacturing, and Paper — good for expanding the list beyond pure F&B/CPG if you want variety.
